# SandboxAI tactical design references

The original SandboxAI layouts use publicly described gameplay characteristics of TTK Testing's Institute, Research Station, and Compound as design references only. No proprietary geometry, code, or assets are used.

- **Facility**: enclosed corridors and rooms feed a central open area. Short and medium lanes, stair transitions, doorway holds, and alternate rotations make corner clearing and cover choice meaningful.
- **Research Complex**: laboratory volumes, catwalk-height changes, balcony positions, and long central lanes support medium/long engagements while side routes prevent a single dominant angle.
- **Compound**: buildings, walls, container-like cover, open ground, and elevated watch positions create multiple approaches and deliberate risk when crossing long sightlines.

All layouts favor readable cover, at least two routes between combat spaces, mixed engagement distances, deterministic spawn selection, and lightweight modular geometry suitable for parallel environments.
